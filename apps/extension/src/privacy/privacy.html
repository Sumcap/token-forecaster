Privacy

Token Forecaster data choices

Forecasting works without telemetry. Both contribution choices are off until you explicitly enable them, and turning them down does not remove any feature.

Reliability diagnostics

If enabled, the extension sends its version, the supported Claude surface, enumerated success or failure stages, and coarse timing buckets. It does not send prompt text, reply text, page URLs, account identifiers, cookies, or arbitrary error stacks.

Forecast research

If separately enabled, the extension derives numeric and categorical features from the draft in the browser, then sends those features, the forecast frozen at send, and an estimated token count of the visible reply. Raw prompts and replies are not sent. Browser outcomes are marked dom_estimate so they cannot be mistaken for provider-exact usage.

Storage, identifiers, and deletion

The collector issues a random credential for this installation and stores only its keyed hash. Events are queued locally while delivery is unavailable. Settings lets you stop either contribution, clear the local queue, and permanently delete this installation’s collected rows. No data is sold or used for advertising.

Before a public release, this packaged notice must also be published at the privacy-policy URL entered in the Chrome Web Store dashboard, together with the operator’s legal identity, contact details, retention period, hosting region, subprocessors, and applicable data rights.
